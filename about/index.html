About

I’m Marcus Körner. Online I’m atla. Leihwelt is the name on the games I make: a one-person indie studio based in Germany.

I’ve been building games since I was 16, around 2000. By day I work as a software engineering manager. Nights and weekends are for shipping worlds, shops, and arcade races.

Now

  • Veilspan — a browser MUD, and the live world for the open-source TalesMUD framework.
  • Pirates Bay Palooza — a 2D pixel-art pirate shopkeeping game in Unity. Public playtest builds for Windows and Android.
  • Neon Velocity — a neon arcade racer in the browser. A Unity port for Xbox Series X is in progress.

mycozy.town is a separate arcade cabinet of smaller toys. This site is the studio, not that cabinet.
