**Veilspan** is the live face of **TalesMUD** — a long-running multiplayer text world I've kept iterating on for years. Players explore, talk, and play in a persistent setting served from modern Go backends.

This is the flagship hobby project: still active, still changing, and open enough that the TalesMUD org on GitHub is the public home for related code and experiments.
