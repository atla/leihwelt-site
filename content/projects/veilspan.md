**Veilspan** is a fantasy adventure in the ruins of **Aethermoor**. You play it in the browser — no telnet client. Explore, talk, fight, and follow quests in a persistent multiplayer world.

It is also the official live world for **[TalesMUD](https://talesmud.io)**, an open-source framework for browser MUDs (Go backend, Svelte client, Lua scripting). The engine is public under the [TalesMUD](https://github.com/TalesMUD/talesmud) org. The Veilspan client that ships on veilspan.com is separate from that public engine.

The lineage is older than the current world: earlier experiments include dungeonsrv and a 2020 MUD called Tales of a Pirate. Veilspan is the game you can play today.
